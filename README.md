# WeMain

**Be the main character. We've got your back.**

WeMain is a hands-free safety and wellbeing companion for women. Inside it lives a personal AI companion (default name *Sassy*, renamable by each user) who:

- keeps her company while she travels alone,
- supports her when she feels low (with crisis routing to 9-8-8 in Canada),
- listens for her code word and escalates to trusted contacts without a single button press,
- notices when her routine looks off and checks in,
- respects a duress phrase that appears to cancel while the alert continues silently.

## Privacy by design
- Audio lives only in a rolling 5-minute in-memory loop. It is never saved or uploaded, except a clip shared with her own contacts during a confirmed alert.
- Settings (name, words, contacts) are stored on her device only.
- No API keys are stored in this repository.
